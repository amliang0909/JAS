### Understanding rMATS 

.

 rMATS uses a hierarchical statistical model to test whether the inclusion levels of alternative splicing events differ significantly between two groups of samples, while accounting for biological variation across replicates. Its key innovation is that, instead of simply pooling replicates together, it models two sources of variation separately — "within-sample sequencing noise" and "between-sample biological fluctuation."
